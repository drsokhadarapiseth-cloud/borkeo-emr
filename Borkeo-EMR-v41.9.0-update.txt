1. SUMMARY OF CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This update fixes the issue where clicking "Register", "Triage", or other department
actions while logged in as Superuser redirected or bounced back to the home page (/home).
Superuser now has full native authority to launch and operate as each role:

  a) Registration Role Operations:
     - When clicking "Register" (/emr?view=register), Superuser directly opens
       Page 1 (កត់ត្រាព័ត៌មានទូទៅ / General Registration) without redirection.
     - Superuser is recognized in the registration scope to enter patient
       demographics, select New/Old PMRS2 case type (ករណីថ្មី / ករណីចាស់), and
       successfully save registration records.
     - Saves are validated and dispatch seamlessly to Triage.

  b) Triage Role Operations:
     - When clicking "Triage" (/triage or /emr?view=triage), Superuser directly
       opens the triage workspace / Page 2 (កត់ត្រាការពិនិត្យដំបូង).
     - Superuser can record vital signs (temperature, pulse, BP, SpO2, respiratory
       rate), triage priority/triage category, and dispatch patients to wards or OPD.

  c) Clinical & Specialty Roles:
     - Superuser has full write permissions across Outpatient consultation,
       Inpatient SOAP, Nursing records, Daily progress notes, and Discharge forms.
     - Direct access without redirect barriers to Billing (/billing), Finance
       (/finance), Expenses (/expenses), and User Administration (/admin/users).

  d) Seamless View Routing:
     - Navigation to /emr with ?view=register automatically activates Page 1.
     - Navigation to /emr with ?view=triage automatically activates Page 2.
     - Navigation to clinical views automatically activates Page 3.
     - Superuser can switch between all tabs (b1, b2, b3) freely without restriction.

  e) Account & Security:
     - ឈ្មោះគណនី (Username): Superuser
     - ពាក្យសម្ងាត់ (Password): 1234
     - Patient data and clinical records are fully preserved without any data loss.
     - 100% Khmer interface compliance (npm run check:khmer passes).
     - Full test suite passes.
