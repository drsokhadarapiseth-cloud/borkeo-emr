BORKEO EMR v40.1.0 - COMBINED UPDATE NOTE
Base: v40.0.0. Clinical, pharmacy, laboratory and financial logic were preserved. Patient records are not touched.

WHAT CHANGED
1. (v40.1.0) Role-based Chart Tabs in Patient Banner:
   - Filtered the 7 chart tabs (សង្ខេប, សញ្ញាជីវិត, បញ្ជា, លទ្ធផល, ឱសថ, កំណត់ត្រា, បេឡា) by active staff role so staff only see what they need for their duties:
     * Management (Admin, Director, Vice Director): All 7 tabs.
     * Clinical Doctors & Ward Heads (Doctor, Ward Head, Chief Ward): Clinical tabs (សង្ខេប, សញ្ញាជីវិត, បញ្ជា, លទ្ធផល, ឱសថ, កំណត់ត្រា) without Cashier (បេឡា).
     * Inpatient Nursing (Nurse, Midwife): Clinical tabs without Cashier (បេឡា).
     * Triage & Registration: សង្ខេប and សញ្ញាជីវិត.
     * Cashier / Billing (Clerk, Accountant): សង្ខេប and បេឡា.
     * Pharmacy: សង្ខេប and ឱសថ.
     * Paraclinic (Lab, Imaging): សង្ខេប, បញ្ជា, and លទ្ធផល.
   - If the previous tab is not allowed for the role, it smoothly defaults to the first permitted tab (សង្ខេប).
   - Re-renders dynamically when active user role changes or when EMR session is loaded.

INSTALL (one step at a time)
  Step 1. Stop the running EMR if active (Ctrl+C in terminal window).
  Step 2. Start the EMR again: double-click Start-Borkeo-WiFi.bat or run `npm start`.
  Step 3. In the browser, press Ctrl+F5 on the EMR page to reload the updated assets.
