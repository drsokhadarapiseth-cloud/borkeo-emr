BORKEO EMR v41.1.0 - COMBINED UPDATE NOTE
Medical record restructure, PHASE 5: display rules by age and sex, and the
paediatric section.
Base: v41.0.0 (Phase 4). Clinical, pharmacy, laboratory and financial logic
was not changed. Patient records are not touched. Hiding a section never
deletes what was written in it.

WHAT CHANGED (IPD medical record, Subjective box)
1. Sections now follow the patient's age and sex:
   - Vaccination list: shown for ages 0-14, hidden from 15.
   - Smoking / alcohol / drug questions: shown from age 10.
   - NEW Paediatric section (ages 0-14): birth history, feeding, growth
     (weight and height from the vital signs, head circumference up to
     age 2, notes), developmental milestones.
   - NEW Tetanus vaccine status (TT1-TT5, no dose, unknown, last dose
     date): women 15-49 and pregnant patients.
   - Obstetric / gynaecological history, your rule:
       women 15 and over: shown;
       girls under 15: shown when pregnant (EDD written) or in the
       obstetric ward; otherwise a "show" button opens it;
       men: never.
   When age or sex is not known, everything is shown. A section that
   already has something written in it is always shown.
2. TB screening: the same block in OPD and IPD. IPD now also has
   "contact with a TB patient".
3. Version 41.1.0.
