1. SUMMARY OF CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Navigation Tabs & Header Restoration (`protected/EMR.html`, `public/emr-permissions.js`):
   - Fully restored `.step-wrapper` (navigation buttons for Page 1 ចុះឈ្មោះ,
     Page 2 ទ្រីយ៉ា, Page 3 ពិនិត្យជំងឺ) and `.dashboard-header`.
   - Navigation is never suppressed, allowing seamless switching across all forms.

2. Clinical Workspace & Banner Integrity (`public/patient-banner.js`, `public/emr-consultation-workspace.js`, `public/patient-banner.css`):
   - Removed `on-registration-page` classification from `isRegistrationUser()`
     so that doctors, nurses, and admins opening `/emr` or viewing Page 1 are not
     falsely identified as registration-only users.
   - Restored persistent patient banner, chart tabs, summary cards, and the
     modern consultation workspace for clinical and administrative staff.

3. Role Permissions Reset (`public/emr-permissions.js`):
   - Explicitly unhides all clinical UI sections when switching to or operating
     under Doctor, Director, Vice Director, or Admin accounts.

4. Testing & Verification:
   - Added automated unit test `tests/v439-restore-navigation-and-workspace.test.mjs`.
   - Verified 100% pass across all tests and verified Khmer compliance.
