BORKEO EMR v41.7.0 - COMBINED UPDATE NOTE
Medical record restructure, PHASE 10 (last phase): record view log and
scanned documents.
Base: v41.6.0. Clinical, pharmacy, laboratory and financial logic was not
changed. Patient records are not touched; two new tables are added.

WHAT CHANGED
1. Record view log:
   - The EMR now keeps a list of every time a patient record is opened,
     printed or exported, and every time a scanned document is opened:
     who, role, ward, date and time, what, and the computer's IP address.
   - Opening the same record again within 2 minutes is one entry.
   - New page in the sidebar (Admin, Director, Vice director only):
     "Record view log". Search by patient, staff, action and dates.
2. Scanned documents:
   - New button next to the forms button in the OPD, IPD and nursing
     records: attach a PDF, JPG or PNG (up to 10 MB) with a title and kind
     (referral letter, lab result, imaging report, signed consent,
     identity document, other).
   - The EMR checks the real file type; a renamed file is refused.
   - Files are kept in the folder data\uploads\patients. BACK UP THIS
     FOLDER together with the database.
   - Doctors, nurses, midwives, ward chiefs, Registration and management
     can attach. "Remove" needs a reason; the file is kept and the entry
     shows as removed.
   - Attaching, opening and removing are written in the audit log.
3. Version 41.7.0.
