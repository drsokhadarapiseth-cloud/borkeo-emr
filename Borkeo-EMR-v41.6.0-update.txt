BORKEO EMR v41.6.0 - COMBINED UPDATE NOTE
Medical record restructure, PHASE 9: discharge, intake/output, early
warning score, critical results.
Base: v41.5.0. Pharmacy, laboratory and financial logic was not changed.
Patient records are not touched; one new table is added.

WHAT CHANGED
1. Discharge form:
   - Main and secondary ICD-10 diagnoses (the main code also goes into the
     existing discharge ICD-10 box, as before).
   - Patient instructions.
   - "Printable discharge summary" button: prints the SAVED discharge with
     the letterhead (diagnoses, outcome, follow-up date, discharge
     medicines, instructions, signature line).
   - All existing discharge fields and outcomes are unchanged.
2. Nursing chart: a new "Daily balance" row under the fluid-out total
   (fluid in minus fluid out). It is calculated automatically.
3. Early Warning Score (NEWS2, your choice):
   - Shown in the IPD record (Objective box) and in the patient banner.
   - New in the Objective box: "on oxygen" and consciousness (A, C, V, P, U).
   - Uses the latest daily note, otherwise the admission vital signs.
   - Not used for patients under 16 or for pregnant women.
   - If a value is missing it shows "8 or more, incomplete".
4. Critical results: a critical lab or imaging result that nobody
   acknowledges within 30 minutes (your choice) is sent to the ward
   doctors, the ward chief and the ward head (messages).
5. Version 41.6.0.
