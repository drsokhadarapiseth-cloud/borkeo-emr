BORKEO EMR v41.4.0 - COMBINED UPDATE NOTE
Medical record restructure, PHASE 8: newborn record and postnatal care.
Base: v41.3.0 (Phase 7). Clinical, pharmacy, laboratory and financial logic
was not changed. Patient records are not touched. The ANC form, delivery
form and labour chart are not changed.

WHAT CHANGED
1. Newborn record (on the mother's record):
   - Apgar score at 1, 5 and 10 minutes, birth weight, length, head
     circumference, resuscitation, immediate newborn care (6 ticks),
     vitamin K, birth vaccines (BCG, hepatitis B birth dose), the baby's
     own case number when registered, notes.
   - Twins or triplets: choose the number of babies; each baby gets its
     own section.
   - Midwife and doctor e-signatures; print button.
2. Linked to the delivery form:
   - A new "Newborn record" button next to the delivery form's print
     button.
   - A new newborn record is filled from the delivery form (time of
     birth, number of babies, baby 1 alive or stillborn, sex, weight).
     The midwife checks and saves.
3. Postnatal care record for the mother (one per day): blood pressure,
   pulse, temperature, uterus, lochia, wound, breasts and breastfeeding,
   danger signs, family planning, iron / folic acid / vitamin A, a baby
   check line, next visit.
4. Both are in the same window as the consents (button at the top of the
   records). They appear only for women.
5. Version 41.4.0.
