BORKEO EMR v41.3.0 - COMBINED UPDATE NOTE
Medical record restructure, PHASE 7: consents, surgery, anaesthesia and
transfusion forms.
Base: v41.2.0 (Phase 6). Clinical, pharmacy, laboratory and financial logic
was not changed. Patient records are not touched; two new tables are added.
The existing treatment agreement (kech sanya) is unchanged.

WHAT CHANGED
1. A new button "Consents, surgery and transfusion" at the top of the OPD,
   IPD and nursing records. It also works when the record is opened from a
   ward bed. It opens a window with nine forms:
   - Procedure consent, Anaesthesia consent, Blood transfusion consent,
     Refusal of treatment form
   - Pre-operative checklist (WHO list: Sign in, Time out, Sign out)
   - Operative note, Anaesthesia record, Post-operative orders
   - Blood transfusion record
2. One form per date (your rule): the same date opens the same form; a new
   date makes a new form. Every change is kept in the history.
3. Signatures:
   - Staff press "Sign with my e-signature" (doctor, surgeon,
     anaesthetist, nurse, second checker).
   - Patient or family: sign on the screen, or take a photo of the ink
     thumbprint, or sign on the printed paper.
   - The "Fingerprint scanner" button is ready but grey until you buy a
     scanner. Show docs\fingerprint-scanner.md to the supplier.
4. Every form has a PRINT button: letterhead, patient details, the form,
   the consent text and the signature lines.
5. Your Khmer consent texts, checklist and labels are used exactly as you
   wrote them in docs\phase7-consent-drafts.md.
6. Version 41.3.0.
