1. WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) ANC, PNC, family planning and cervical screening were OPD doctor consultations;
   the midwife did not have her own maternal services queue. The vaccination
   service was missing. Safe abortion was in OPD instead of the Gynecology ward.
b) The ANC form (#sub_anc) did not match the official 2026 ANC record pages 2-5;
   it had no colour code, no visit schedule, no auto-fill from Triage, no GA
   from the LMP, no link between the rapid-test results and the lab table, and
   it created one record per visit instead of one per pregnancy.
c) The midwife had no inbox and no way to send a cervical result to a doctor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT CHANGED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) Routing: ANC (anc), PNC (pnc), family planning (family-planning) and cervical
   screening (cervix-screening) go Registration -> Triage -> Cashier -> midwife
   queue. Vaccination (vaccination) goes Triage -> midwife directly (free, no
   Cashier). Safe abortion (safe-abortion) goes to the Gynecology ward queue
   instead of the OPD doctor; the old safe abortion forms stay readable. Reports
   count all of them as OPD services.
b) New page "បញ្ជីអ្នកជំងឺសេវាមាតា" (maternal services queue, /maternal-queue) for
   MIDWIFE of the Obstetric ward (public/nav-config.js group care, server/
   access.mjs roleCanPage): today's paid ANC / PNC / family planning / cervical
   screening patients and vaccination patients, with a call button like the OPD
   queue. Opening a patient opens the right form. Real midwife accounts and the
   Superuser switched to Midwife both see it.
c) Official ANC record (/anc-record, protected/anc-record.html, public/
   anc-record.js) one per pregnancy, linked to the patient's Health ID, opened
   at the first ANC visit and reused at every visit until delivery, referral to
   CPA3 or closing. The old ANC form opens read-only with a link to the new
   record. Saved values never move: the old records stay exactly as they were.

   Pages 2-5 of the 2026 official record (same order, same Khmer labels, same
   tables): identity, LMP, EDD (LMP + 280 days), EDD by first-trimester
   ultrasound, height, weight and BMI before pregnancy; general, surgical, drug
   allergy (linked to the EMR's structured allergy record), family,
   gynaecological and social history; risk factors; mouth and teeth check;
   anaemia check; previous pregnancies table with the 10 complications of the
   page-2 footnote; lab results table by date (HIV, Syphilis, HBsAg, Anti HCV,
   Hb, HCT, PLT, Group/RH, 3 free rows); ultrasound table (DATE, GA, presenta-
   tion, BPD, placenta, AFI, weight, EDD, other); visit table (DATE, GA, UH,
   FP, FHR, BP&P, Weight, WP, Proteinuria, Glucosuria, Varices, Fer, Vaccina-
   tion, Chief complaint, Diagnosis and intervention, Appointment, NAME).

   Khmer labels from server/catalogues/anc-record-labels.json (transcribed
   exactly from the page images; uncertain words are listed in docs/
   anc-khmer-to-confirm.md for the owner to check against the paper record).

   Print pages 2-5 in the official A4 landscape layout with the colour box
   ticked (public/anc-record.css print rules).
d) Auto-fill (always editable by the midwife; the original values are saved and
   never overwritten): name, date of birth and age from Registration; weight,
   height, BP, pulse, temperature and SpO2 from the Triage of the same visit;
   BMI, weight gain, GA and EDD calculated; midwife name from the login; tetanus
   vaccination from the vaccination page (TT1 / TT2 appears in the visit table
   when the patient is pregnant). Released Laboratory results fill the lab table
   and released Imaging reports the ultrasound table; the midwife's own rapid
   tests (HIV, syphilis, Hb) are typed in the visit form and also appear in the
   lab table.
e) GA and EDD: from the LMP (EDD = LMP + 280 days). A dating ultrasound before
   14 weeks whose EDD differs by more than 7 days from the LMP EDD is used
   instead; the source is always shown (public/anc-engine.mjs).
f) Colour code from server/catalogues/anc-color-chart.json (copied unchanged
   from borkeo-update-files/anc/, 69 items in 4 sections): the "auto" items
   decide from the data (age, height, BMI, BP, previous pregnancies, Hb,
   syphilis, HIV, twins), the others are tick boxes by the midwife. The highest
   level wins (red > yellow > green); the result is re-checked at every visit
   and every save. The official boxes បៃតង / លឿង / ក្រហម show at the top of the
   record and as a badge in the patient banner on every page; every colour
   change is kept with its date, reasons and the midwife who saved it.
g) Visit schedule from server/catalogues/anc-visit-schedule.json (copied
   unchanged from borkeo-update-files/anc/, green 4 visits page 7, yellow and
   red 8 visits page 8): which visit this is by GA and colour, its tasks
   (records, lab, exam, ultrasound, care) as check items, overdue tasks from
   earlier visits in red, and "មិនមែនជាការពិនិត្យតាមកាលវិភាគ" / "Not a scheduled
   visit" between the visits. The to-do list is a side panel always visible and
   also opens once when the record opens.

   A task acts: the midwife's own tests (HIV, syphilis, Hb, urine) open the
   result field; HBsAg / OGTT / CBC / ultrasound create the paraclinic order
   (patient pays at the Cashier and the order goes to the Laboratory or
   Imaging); iron and folic acid add the prescription; tetanus vaccine opens
   the vaccination entry (free, direct, the dose also appears in the visit
   table). A task can be closed undone only with a reason: មិនមានតេស្ត /
   អ្នកជំងឺបដិសេធ / ធ្វើរួចនៅកន្លែងផ្សេង / ផ្សេងៗ (text).

   Next appointment: suggested from the date of the next scheduled visit for
   the colour; the midwife can change it.
h) Yellow shows "ANC continues at Borkeo Referral Hospital (CPA2), 8 visits".
   Red shows the referral button: a DOCTOR or CHIEF_WARD of Obstetric or
   Gynecology signs it with the existing e-signature; the referral letter prints
   (public/anc-referral-letter.mjs: letterhead, patient, GA, colour and red
   reasons, vital signs, test results, treatment given, referring doctor
   signature line); the visit is marked "referred to CPA3" for reports. The
   layout is kept easy to replace by the official form later (the owner will
   upload it).
i) Midwife permissions: she opens and saves the forms of these patients
   (server/encounter-access.mjs: canOpenRecord, canSaveRecord by service code);
   orders HBsAg from the Laboratory and ultrasound from Imaging and nothing else
   (server/routes/paraclinic.mjs: MIDWIFE allowed for HBsAg and ultrasound
   orders only); prescribes only ferrous salt, ferrous salt + folic acid, folic
   acid, mebendazole, calcium and the vaccines of the vaccination service (the
   medicine search is filtered by role and service for MIDWIFE in public/
   medicine-autocomplete.js and checked on the server in server/
   pharmacy-prescriptions.mjs). The list of allowed medicines is in
   server/clinical-options.json midwifeAllowedMedicines and is easy to change.
j) Vaccination page (/vaccination, protected/vaccination.html, public/
   vaccination.js) for all ages, free: the vaccine is chosen from the medicine
   list lines marked vaccine (no filter; the national schedule vaccines are in
   the list), with dose number, date, batch number, injection site, next
   appointment and reaction. Each dose is deducted from stock (server/
   maternal.mjs: same stock movement as a dispensed medicine, reference = the
   vaccination id); the same vaccine and dose number twice gives a warning.
   Tetanus given to a pregnant woman also appears in the visit table of her ANC
   record (the TT1 and TT2 dose numbers). Vaccination history per Health ID with
   the next appointment; a vaccination card section prints.
k) Cervical screening: the midwife saves the result in the OPD consultation
   (server/routes/patients.mjs checks the role, no other role can use this
   endpoint) and it is sent as a staff message to the Gynecology ward doctors'
   inbox (existing staff messages) with the patient link; the doctor's
   acknowledgement completes the message. The send button no longer needs the
   required fields of the whole OPD consultation to be filled first: it saves
   nothing into the record (data-required-bypass in public/maternal-emr.js), it
   checks its own result field, and the server checks the role before accepting
   the save.
l) Safe abortion is billed under the Gynecology ward (server/finance.mjs
   departmentForServiceCode: safe-abortion -> gynecology). Old safe abortion
   records stay readable.
m) Defaults the owner may change later are kept together in server/
   clinical-options.json (chart gaps for age 18-19 and 35, height under 145 cm,
   BMI under 18.5, BP, number of births, twins as red; the visit window of
   +/- 1 week for the single-week visits 26, 36, 38 and 40; the paid and free
   service codes; the midwife's medicines).
n) Khmer: the record, colour chart and visit schedule text is copied from
   borkeo-update-files/anc/ and never retyped from memory; the words that could
   not be read with certainty on the page images are listed for the owner in
   docs/anc-khmer-to-confirm.md. Other new texts are in docs/
   translations-needed.csv marked "draft Khmer, owner to review".

4. INSTALLATION (one step at a time)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The files are already updated in this folder. If you use this folder as is,
only steps 1 to 4 are needed.

Step 1: Close the EMR server window (or press Ctrl + C in it).
Step 2: Double-click "Start-Borkeo-WiFi.bat". At the first start the new tables
        (ANC pregnancy record, ANC visit tasks) are added to the database. No
        patient data and no old ANC form is touched.
Step 3: In the browser press Ctrl + F5 once. Triage now offers ANC, PNC, family
        planning, cervical screening, vaccination and safe abortion.
Step 4: Log in as the midwife (or as the Superuser and switch to Midwife). The
        new menu item "បញ្ជីអ្នកជំងឺសេវាមាតា" appears. Open a patient and the
        official ANC record opens; the to-do side panel shows the tasks of the
        current visit.

For Antigravity: this folder already contains the update. To get it on another
computer, run "git pull" in the project folder, then the steps above, then
"npm test" and "npm run check:khmer". The full code change is the Git commit
"v44.3.0" on master (git show v44.3.0 or git log).
