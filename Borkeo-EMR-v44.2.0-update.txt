1. WHAT WAS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) The medicine search showed every form and strength of every medicine at
   once ("par" gave a long list of paracetamol lines and other medicines), and
   it showed medicines that do not fit the patient (adult tablets for a small
   child, oxytocin for a man, newborn caffeine for an adult).
b) The medicine list file had the other name in brackets in the name and no
   marks for children, adults, women only or newborns only.
c) The route list had six different injection choices; doctors chose them
   differently for the same medicine.
d) The maximum-dose table was empty: no dose was checked, for adults or for
   children. A total with no number of medicine showed 0.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT CHANGED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) Medicine list: the owner's new file (704 lines, 339 medicine names, other
   names, route, adult / child / women-only / newborn-only marks, age limits,
   notes) replaces the list of v42.3.
b) Two-step search in every prescription form (OPD, IPD admission and protocol
   rows, daily note, discharge medicines):
   - step 1: each medicine name once (name, other names, CMS code).
     "para" shows only Paracetamol; "acetam" also finds it; "cotrim" finds
     Sulfamethoxazole + trimethoprim.
   - step 2: the forms and strengths of that name for this patient, as
     "CMS code · Form · Strength"; a note shows as an orange line, for example
     "⚠ កុមារ៖ Children > 6 months". One allowed line is chosen automatically.
   - a full CMS code (e.g. AA0410) goes straight to its line.
   - arrows and Enter in both steps; Backspace from step 2 back to the names;
     Esc closes; no match: រកមិនឃើញឱសថ. Free text is still allowed.
   - after choosing: CMS-Name-Form-Strength in the name box, the number of
     medicine is cleared and gets the cursor, the unit is set. The route is
     never filled.
c) Filter by the open patient: child (0-14 years, or Pediatric ward / NCU) sees
   child lines, adult sees adult lines, unknown age sees everything. Women-only
   lines: women from 15 years, or a pregnant girl (EDD), Obstetric / Gynecology
   ward, ANC, PNC or family planning; never a man. Newborn-only lines: under 28
   days or NCU. Lines with an age or weight limit are shown with the orange
   note. A saved prescription is never changed by the filter.
d) Routes for a NEW line: ចាក់សរសៃ (IV), ចាក់សាច់ដុំ (IM), ចាក់ក្រោមស្បែក (SC),
   លេប (PO), លាប, បន្តក់, ស្រូប, តាមទ្វារមាស, តាមទ្វារធំ (PR), ក្រោមអណ្តាត (SL).
   Old lines with the old injection values open unchanged and are shown as
   IV / IM (also in Pharmacy, staff messages, printing). Nothing saved is
   rewritten; PMRS2 import still accepts the old values.
e) Maximum dose check (adult and child): the draft table (395 lines) is loaded
   into the drug safety table. Only lines APPROVED by the Head of Pharmacy or
   the Director are used. Page "Drug safety tables" (/admin/drug-safety, also
   opened from the Pharmacy dispensing page): filters; the administrator edits
   (the line goes back to draft); the Head of Pharmacy or the Director clicks
   Approve or Reject (name and time saved). When a doctor or midwife enters a
   dose, and again on save:
   - dose = number x strength (tablets x mg; mL x mg per mL; mg as typed),
     per day = per dose x doses per day (QD, BID, TID, QID, qH);
   - adult: maximum per dose / per day, and per kg x Triage weight when given
     (the lower one); child: per kg x weight, never above the child maximum;
   - child without a weight: orange note បញ្ចូលទម្ងន់ ដើម្បីពិនិត្យកម្រិតថ្នាំ;
   - above the maximum: red warning with the dose and the maximum; to continue
     the prescriber writes the reason; it is saved with the line and in the
     audit. Without the reason the record is not saved.
   - a dose that cannot be converted gives no warning; it is listed for the
     pharmacist on the drug safety page.
f) Totals stay empty until the number of medicine is typed.
g) Khmer: the drug safety page and the warning window now show Khmer. Texts the
   owner did not give are marked "draft Khmer, owner to review" in
   docs/translations-needed.csv.

4. INSTALLATION (one step at a time)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The files are already updated in this folder. If you use this folder as is,
only steps 1 to 4 are needed.

Step 1: Close the EMR server window (or press Ctrl + C in it).
Step 2: Double-click "Start-Borkeo-WiFi.bat". At the first start the draft
        maximum-dose table is added to the database (all lines "draft").
Step 3: In the browser press Ctrl + F5 once, open a patient's prescription and
        type "par" in a medicine name box: one name, Paracetamol. Press Enter
        to see its forms.
Step 4: The Head of Pharmacy logs in, opens Pharmacy dispensing, clicks
        "តារាងសុវត្ថិភាពឱសថ" and approves the lines that are correct
        (the Director can do the same). Until a line is approved, that
        medicine is not checked.

For Antigravity: this folder already contains the update. To get it on another
computer, run "git pull" in the project folder, then steps 1 to 4 above, then
"npm test" and "npm run check:khmer". The full code change is the Git commit
"v44.2.0" on master (git show HEAD).
