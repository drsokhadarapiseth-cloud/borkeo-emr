BORKEO EMR v37.8 - PATIENT LIST ACTIONS, ENCOUNTER PERMISSIONS AND MANAGEMENT VIEWER
BASE: approved package 37.7.0 in the user's current Borkeo_EMR_v37.1 project folder.

APPROVED BEHAVIOR
Patient-list View and Edit are independent actions. Registration opens only Registration; Triage opens only Triage. Existing records open read-only, with Khmer Edit Information and Save buttons on the form for an explicit edit and in-place save. Saving checks required fields and returns to read-only. Completed Triage edits preserve the assigned ward and completed workflow; changing the ward uses the existing transfer process.
OPD doctors see OPD; IPD doctors see their IPD form. OPD clinical edits stop after Pharmacy dispensing/receipt. IPD clinical edits stop after discharge and require a management-approved correction. Nurses and Midwives follow these encounter locks for Nursing Care only. Chief of Ward uses the ward-doctor permissions. The existing daily medicine list and its ordered six-signature approval workflow are preserved.
Cashier invoice history shows paid/settled invoices. Correction and invoice cancellation require management approval. A correction approval belongs to its requester and current invoice snapshot and is used once. Approved cancellation retains charges and receipts as audited voided rows. Direct paid-invoice editing and direct payment reversal are blocked.
Every patient-list trash action submits a reason and a management request. No patient-list user, including management, directly deletes a record. Approved deletion uses the existing deletion/audit routine for the selected encounter and leaves other visits with the same Patient ID intact.
Admin, Vice Director and Director have the same record-management process. Management View opens a separate record viewer with OPD/IPD selection and an encounter selector. IPD has seven logical pages: Registration; Triage; Medical Record with laboratory results; all daily follow-ups with laboratory results; Nursing Care; Discharge; and a structured admission-to-discharge resume built from recorded facts. Page 4 includes all follow-ups and scrolls. The viewer creates static read-only form snapshots and disposes its live source chart after rendering. It does not save, sign, route or complete a patient record.
The Registration payment cell uses one category line without a second payment-status badge. HEF, direct payment, waiver and discount have the agreed Khmer labels. Existing NSSF, insurance and NGO payer categories remain distinct; this display update does not reclassify coverage or change bills.
All new interface labels are Khmer; laboratory names remain English. The earlier removal of EN buttons and required-asterisk validation remain enabled.

IMPLEMENTATION AND DATA
Server authorization enforces role, ward, form scope, Pharmacy receipt and discharge locks, including forged save scopes. Closed-IPD correction grants are bound to the requester, form scope and patient version, and consumed in the patient-save transaction. Existing optimistic version checks remain active. Form serialization preserves other forms' recorded values. Embedded specialty forms cannot autosave from View mode.
Startup adds only the encounter_corrections authorization table and an operation column to finance_invoice_edit_requests when missing. Existing requests, patient data, images, laboratory results, clinical forms, prescriptions, financial calculations and daily-drug signatures are not replaced. This update includes source and tests only, with no database, .env, data directory or patient-photo payload.
