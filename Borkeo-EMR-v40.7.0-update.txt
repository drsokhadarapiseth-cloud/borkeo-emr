BORKEO EMR v40.7.0 - COMBINED UPDATE NOTE
Medical record restructure, PHASE 1: IPD admission record in SOAP order.
Base: v40.6.0 (Phase 0). Clinical, pharmacy, laboratory and financial logic
and permissions were not changed. Patient records are not touched.

WHAT CHANGED
1. The doctor's IPD admission record now has four coloured boxes:
   S  Subjective  - reason for admission, present illness, other complaints
                    (TB screening), current medicines, past history
                    (immunization, drug allergy, obstetric/gynaecological,
                    family, community)
   O  Objective   - vital signs, physical examination (with the "mark all
                    normal" KH/EN/FR button), investigations
   A  Assessment  - provisional diagnosis, differential diagnosis, ICD-10,
                    prognosis
   P  Plan        - drug treatment, other interventions, diet, monitoring,
                    and a NEW "Plan notes" box
   Each box can be folded and shows a dot: grey = empty, green = filled.
   The title, first-examination time and doctor signature stay where they were.
2. Main and secondary ICD-10 (IPD):
   - The first code is marked as the main diagnosis.
   - The others are listed as secondary diagnoses, each with a
     "set as main" button.
   - PMRS2, discharge and reports use the main code.
   - Old records keep their first code as the main one.
3. Your Khmer text is unchanged. The only change: the numbers in front of
   seven section titles were removed (the titles now sit inside S/O/A/P).
   All seven are listed in docs/khmer-approved-changes.md.
4. Old records are safe. The fields that were saved by their position on
   the page keep their old position. Tested with fake records saved in the
   current format and in the oldest format: all 103 fields open in the right
   box and save back unchanged.
5. Print: still two A4 pages at the same text size, now with the four
   S/O/A/P sections. The prescription sheet is unchanged.
6. Version 40.7.0.
