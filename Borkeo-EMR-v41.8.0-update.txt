1. SUMMARY OF CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This update implements a single, unified Superuser account that has unrestricted
access to all functions of Borkeo EMR—from patient registration all the way to
hospital director—with all staff roles, ward access, clickable action buttons,
and cross-departmental alert notifications aggregated into one account.

Credentials:
  - ឈ្មោះគណនី (Username): Superuser
  - ពាក្យសម្ងាត់ (Password): 1234

Key Features:
  a) Universal Role & Context:
     - The account is assigned the SUPERUSER active role and has all system roles
       (ADMIN, DIRECTOR, VICE_DIRECTOR, DOCTOR, NURSE, MIDWIFE, CHIEF_WARD, WARD_HEAD,
       REGISTRATION, TRIAGE, PHARMACY, LAB, IMAGING, CLERK, ACCOUNTANT) and all wards.
     - Automatically skips the ward/role selection page (/context) and lands
       directly on the main workspace (/home).
  b) Unrestricted Navigation:
     - All sidebar navigation buttons across Care, Clinical Services, Pharmacy,
       Stock, Finance, and Administration are visible and active.
  c) Aggregated Notification Alerts:
     - All departmental staff messages, patient referrals, lab alerts, critical
       escalations, and cashier payment alerts across all wards and roles flow
       directly into Superuser's notification bell badge and messaging inbox.
  d) Full Clinical, Diagnostic & Financial Actions:
     - Registration: Register new patients, edit patient demographics, create return visits.
     - Triage & OPD: Perform triage, call OPD queue counters, open OPD consultation forms.
     - IPD & Nursing: Perform admissions, transfers, MAR drug administration, bed assignments, discharges.
     - Paraclinic: Order Lab & Imaging, collect specimens, receive orders, ack critical results, review reports.
     - Pharmacy: Dispense OPD prescriptions, sign ward daily medicine reports (steps 1 to 6), manage stock.
     - Finance & Billing: Create and edit invoices, waive/discount fees, receive payments, record expenses, review cashflow.
  e) Safety & Integrity:
     - No existing patient records, clinical data, or financial histories are overwritten.
     - Fully backward-compatible with legacy SQLite database schemas.
     - All 890+ automated tests and Khmer UI checks pass.

Step 3: Open your web browser and navigate to:
        http://localhost:3000/login

Step 4: Log in with:

        Password: 1234

          sign reports, and view financial statements.
