BORKEO EMR v41.2.0 - COMBINED UPDATE NOTE
Medical record restructure, PHASE 6: medication administration record and
prescribing warnings.
Base: v41.1.0 (Phase 5). Pharmacy, laboratory and financial logic was not
changed. Patient records are not touched; four new tables are added.

IMPORTANT - BUG FIXED (it is in the version you are running now)
  In the nursing medicine chart, the tick "medicine given" was NOT saved.
  It disappeared when the record was opened again. Now every dose is saved.

WHAT CHANGED
1. Medication Administration Record (nurses and midwives):
   - Click a dose in the nursing medicine chart. A form opens:
     Given / Held / Refused, time, reason or note, and your signature.
   - Held or Refused needs a reason.
   - The chart shows the result in the dose box. The full list is under
     the chart and prints with the nursing form.
   - A mistake is corrected with "Correct": the old entry stays, crossed
     out, with the reason. Nothing is deleted.
   - It uses the doctor's existing treatment orders (admission
     prescription and daily notes).
2. Prescribing warnings (doctors), when a medicine is typed in the
   admission, daily note or OPD prescription:
   - Allergy warning (from the allergy record of Phase 4).
   - Duplicate warning (same medicine twice in the prescription).
   - Interaction and maximum dose warnings, from two tables that are
     EMPTY until you fill them.
   To continue, write the override reason (it is saved and audited), or
   press "Do not prescribe".
3. New admin page in the menu (administration): drug safety tables, for
   interactions and maximum doses. Only an administrator can change them.
4. Version 41.2.0.
